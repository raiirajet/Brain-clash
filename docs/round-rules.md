# Round duration and game contracts

Training always honors the selected active-play duration (or manual end for unlimited); completed arcade objectives restart a fresh stage without deleting earned points. Boss training restarts after a defeat. The single-player renderer has one lane, no phantom orange opponent, and XP is not a percent. Competitive goal modes end at the goal or maximum duration. Competitive special arcade modes also support full-duration repeat stages. The timed winner compares completed stages plus current progress. Sprint no longer changes the selected duration. Final Clash may continue after the timer only on a tie; its deciding question is explicit. Number Bomb mistakes/expiry restart the individual fuse after cooldown. The result displays finish reason and active elapsed time. Time is bounded at the configured deadline. Automated tests cover all 44 training variants, all eight competitive rules, four durations, all four digit and difficulty levels, and independent speed ties. Physical device testing remains required.
