Play. Think. Win.

Brain Clash combines short arcade games with maths, logic, memory and reaction challenges. Play against friends and family on the same device, practise alone or take on a boss together.

Choose arithmetic operations, number ranges and individual difficulty. Unlock fighters and costumes by playing, explore arenas and track your learning progress. Local tournaments and daily and weekly goals keep the action varied.

No ads, no account and no internet connection needed to play. Progress stays on your device.
